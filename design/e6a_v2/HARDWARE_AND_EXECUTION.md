## Profile once, freeze a COMMON batch
Independent jobs must not independently select different paired schedules. Profile every condition/device actually selected by the reviewed plan using actual model sizes, context128, precision, optimizer and feature capture. Committed 3090 evidence establishes C1=16 and C2/C3/C4=8 safe maxima. Committed 4080 evidence establishes C0 safe at 8 within the relevant bound and C1/C2/C5/C6 safe at 4. The corrected solver independently derives common microbatch 4 and accumulation 16 from these measured profiles. This is a measured batch-plan candidate; it is not a final hardware or production lock while real data, calibration and the 3090 environment handoff remain open. The C5 microbatch-4 repeat met the registered headroom criterion with a narrow 15,119,156-byte surplus over that threshold; retain this caveat without adding a new post-hoc threshold.

Try divisors of64 largest-first:64,32,16,8,4,2,1, stopping a pair's search after its largest safe candidate has been established. A valid common proof needs a measured safe candidate at or above the proposed common value for every scheduled pair, the registered headroom rule passed at the proposed value for every pair, and a measured failure of the next larger globally relevant divisor on at least one scheduled pair. Smaller candidates after a safe result are unnecessary. Each measured candidate runs in a fresh isolated subprocess to avoid OOM allocator contamination. Complete warmup and at least two optimizer updates so Adam states exist; include scheduled evaluation with actual resident-model policy and a checkpoint serialization pass. Synchronize; record allocated/reserved peaks, free/total VRAM, other device processes, host RAM and walltime. Retain failed-candidate evidence. Forward-only fit is insufficient.

Choose the largest candidate passing EVERY required profile, equivalently the minimum of their safe maxima. Set common accumulation=64/microbatch and freeze a single hardware-plan hash for all paired S1 seeds/conditions/devices. Spare3090 memory does not authorize different microbatching. A separate architecture pair may have its own common plan.

Proposed headroom is max(1.5GiB,10% physical VRAM), approved and tested under the actual desktop/display workload. Both free memory and peak reservation matter. Evaluation may use a separately validated smaller fixed batch because it does not affect optimizer batching; its reductions must remain equivalent.

If microbatch1 fails, validate common activation-checkpointing/chunk policy and reprofile BEFORE production. Do not silently shorten context, reduce relation heads, drop Q/K/V types/layers, quantize teacher or adopt an8-bit optimizer. These change the numerical/scientific design. Missing profiles block production. CPU smoke mode remains available.

A mid-run OOM safely aborts; no automatic shrink/retry with changed accumulation. Diagnose competing processes and preserve evidence. A protocol-changing fix creates a new lock and reruns affected paired comparisons. One training process per GPU; no DDP/FSDP/DeepSpeed needed.
