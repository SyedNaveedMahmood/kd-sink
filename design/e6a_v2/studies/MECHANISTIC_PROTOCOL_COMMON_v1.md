## Statistical and scientific rules proposed for approval

Primary complementary endpoints: signed edited-minus-clean CE in nats per valid
shifted target and full-vocabulary KL(clean || edited), temperature1. Supporting
outcomes: flips, absolute target-logprob changes, sink, actual parameter/activation
doses, geometry and layer factors. Match the exact items/masks/valid target counts.
Behavior is token weighted; structure/dose is item weighted; factors explicitly
pool query support and heads. Report both populations without silently changing
the support to remove small or opposite effects.

Primary temporal contrast is C2 500 versus10000;2000 is an intermediate
descriptive point. C3/C6 test high-sink objectives, C1/C5 are prescribed controls.
Teacher versus student comparisons retain the same items and explicit scopes.
Propose descriptive reporting only: paired item records, token-weighted effects
and paired contrasts, item distribution median/IQR/p90. No hypothesis tests,
confidence intervals, automatic equivalence/onset/mediation decisions or claims
about variation across training seeds. Optional document-clustered bootstrap
requires a future approval/amendment and a connected-document cluster definition.

Keep outcomes as separate components in their original units. No mixed-unit
composite fingerprint, cosine of zero-response vectors, outcome-fitted scaling
or search for the endpoint most favorable to inheritance/divergence. For each
component, show the complete registered trajectory and teacher reference; classify
monotonic convergence/divergence only if all successive absolute-gap changes
have the same corresponding sign, otherwise mixed/nonmonotonic or insufficient
when support is missing. Exact zeros are zeros, not evidence for route absence.

affected run. A failed bundle cannot contribute to a complete report. Dose
support missing or ambiguous makes that comparison unavailable; it is not a
null result. Keep partial failure evidence; no tolerance or estimand changes
after scientific outcomes. Missing practical-effect margins preclude equivalence.
