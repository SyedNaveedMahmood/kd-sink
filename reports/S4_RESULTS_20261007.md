## Findings in brief

The three high-sink students C2, C3, and C6 had first-position sink mass `S` of 0.332, 0.395, and 0.358 at step 10,000. Replacing the position-0 embedding with position-1's embedding reduced their `S` by 0.304, 0.376, and 0.332, respectively, and increased next-token cross-entropy (CE) by 0.348, 0.328, and 0.212 nats per target. C0, C1, C4, and C5 had much smaller baseline sinks and smaller, but generally nonzero, position-shift effects. The anchor pattern is associated with measured output sensitivity; it does not establish that the sink improves ordinary clean prediction or that one unique circuit mediates the effect.

The time course is important. C2/C3/C6 already had `S` between 0.388 and 0.454 at step 100, yet position-shift `ΔCE` was at most 0.048 nats/target there, and C6's was slightly negative. Their endpoint `ΔCE` was substantially larger. Thus an established sink pattern and a strong behavioral effect of this specific perturbation do not appear at the same measured time. The study did not define a threshold for a binary functional-onset claim.

The GPT-2-large teacher responded substantially to position shift, Q-bias removal, EPE transport, and selected K-coordinate ablation. The student Q-bias and EPE output effects at 10,000 were small, even in high-sink C2/C3/C6. This limits evidence for inheritance of those *specific* routes. Conversely, C5 had the largest endpoint top-three K-coordinate `ΔCE` (0.0377) despite a small baseline sink (0.0242), so this route probe is not a simple monotonic proxy for sink magnitude. Neither a weak response nor a large response alone identifies all available mechanisms.

## Design, units, and scope

The [S4 design](../design/e6a_v2/studies/S4_MECHANISTIC_INHERITANCE.md) and [intervention contract](../design/e6a_v2/MODEL_AND_INTERVENTION_CONTRACTS.md) define ten probes per checkpoint: position-0 to position-1 embedding replacement; removal of *all* absolute positional embeddings; Q-bias zeroing in all native layers; legacy-form first-MLP EPE direction transport at layer 0; top-three model-local K-input-coordinate ablation across all native layers; and five deterministic, matched-cardinality random-coordinate controls. The controls' RNG seed `20260927` is **not** a training seed. K-coordinate identities are separately selected at each model/checkpoint, not mapped teacher-to-student coordinates. The EPE edit conserves the two affected MLP-output vectors' sum, not their norms. Absolute-position removal is a broad intervention, not a selective sink deletion. The registered probe version is `s4-gpt2-route-v1`.

`S` is mean later-query attention mass assigned to key position 0 across native layers and heads. `ΔS = S_edited − S_clean`; negative means the probe reduced the sink. `ΔCE = CE_edited − CE_clean` in nats per valid shifted target; positive means worse target likelihood. Flip fraction is the proportion of valid targets whose top-1 prediction changed. Self-KL compares the clean output distribution to the edited one. Mean absolute target-log-probability change preserves effects that a signed `ΔCE` could cancel. Each probe summary also retains edited accuracy, per-item median and 90th-percentile absolute changes, and guarded sink ratios; see the [metric contract](../design/e6a_v2/METRICS_AND_ANALYSIS.md). Ratios near a tiny baseline are not treated as substantive evidence. These are perturbation measurements, not estimates of a causal mediation fraction.
