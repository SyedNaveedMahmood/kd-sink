# S2: ordinary pretraining development

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-10-07 UTC. **Scientific status:** complete for the prospectively amended common-intersection campaign. Six Pythia trajectories, 918 Dense64 checkpoint evaluations, and 42 Full300 anchor evaluations passed the final independent audit. This report reads existing results; it does not add model evaluations.

## Findings in brief

Across all six trajectories, clean next-token cross-entropy fell sharply between initialization and native step 143,000. The recorded first-key attention mass (`S`) rose from about `0.011` at step 0 to `0.083–0.115` in the three 160m series and `0.147–0.305` in the three 410m series. At the final Full300 anchor, deleting or relocating attention to the first key increased cross-entropy in every series. The six final delete effects range from `0.04725` to `0.06287` nats per target; relocation effects range from `0.06098` to `0.10009`.

Pattern and intervention cost do not have a simple one-to-one ranking. At step 143,000, the 410m seed2 series has lower `S` than 410m seed1 (`0.14728` versus `0.30463`) but a larger relocation cost (`0.10009` versus `0.07573` nats per target). Those two series use the same frozen tokenizer group and raw-document identities. This is a descriptive within-family observation, not evidence that sink mass causes the difference.

## Design, coverage, and denominators

S2 is an observational reference for ordinary pretraining development. It evaluates public EleutherAI Pythia-160m and Pythia-410m checkpoints for three **pretraining** seeds (`1234`, `1`, `2`), with evaluator RNG seed `0` as separate metadata. The scientific source was frozen at commit `d5e0f7a836298c625e2814e5f3aa9b38f5922e80`. Execution used AbdullahPC / `DESKTOP-Q32BHOR`, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 UUID `GPU-a21766e4-bb31-9b79-5e8f-e58021e9708e`, FP32 CUDA, context 128, eager native GPT-NeoX attention, and all native layers. The 160m/410m step-0 engineering qualifications passed before the scientific campaign; the 410m qualification measured 24 native layers, exact logit/attention/no-op parity, mask behavior, intervention response, restoration, and verified resume.

The common-intersection grid contains steps `0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512`, then every 1,000 from 1,000 through 143,000 **except 111,000**. The public `pythia-410m-seed2` step-111,000 revision lacked a valid model/config payload. The prospective decision excluded that step from all six trajectories, leaving **153 steps per trajectory** and **918 distinct series/checkpoint states**. No neighboring or latest checkpoint replaced it. The inventory records native step and training tokens at 2,097,152 tokens per native update; step 143,000 corresponds to 299,892,736,000 documented training tokens.

Dense64 evaluates **64 frozen raw OWT documents at every step**; Full300 evaluates **300 documents** at steps `0, 128, 1,000, 10,000, 50,000, 100,000, 143,000`. Dense64 is nested in Full300. Both use a prospective S2-specific panel: the first 300 unique eligible normalized texts in the verified evaluation-document order within the frozen OWT source window `[400000, 408000)`, deduplicated by exact normalized-text SHA-256. The same raw-document identities are independently tokenized with each pinned Pythia tokenizer. The historical S1 panel instead consists of packed GPT-2 token blocks and cannot be treated as a one-to-one 64/300-document match. Every evaluated S2 item had 128 real tokens, yielding **8,128 valid shifted targets** per Dense64 aggregate and **38,100** per Full300 aggregate.

`S` is the equal-item mean of attention mass on key 0 from second-half queries, averaged over native heads and layers. Clean CE is next-token negative log-likelihood in nats per valid target; PPL is `exp(CE)`. Signed `ΔCE = CE_edited − CE_clean`; positive values mean the intervention worsened target likelihood. Delete redistributes the first-key attention mass over remaining valid keys; relocate moves it to key 1. Both act across all native layers. Self-KL and prediction flips provide complementary output-response measurements. Teacher KL and GPT-2 absolute-position probes are inapplicable to S2.

## Interpretation limits

S2 is an external observational pretraining reference. Pythia size, architecture, tokenizer, data and optimization differ from S1, and equal numerical steps do not mean equal training-token exposure. The four tokenizer content identities mean raw CE/PPL comparisons **across tokenizer groups** are not direct language-model quality comparisons, even though the raw documents match. Within-series changes are the clearest developmental description. Each series has one pretraining seed; 153 checkpoints and 300 documents do not create independent pretraining replicates. No p-value, confidence interval, formal onset threshold, equivalence claim, or mediation claim is made here. The missing public step 111,000 remains excluded for all six series.

The report's tables were computed read-only from the audited external aggregates. For each expected `(series, native step, panel)`, only the aggregate whose `panel_hash` equals the final audit's approved per-series panel binding was admitted; this yields exactly 960 unique keys. The `S` columns were recomputed as arithmetic means of `clean_structure.native_layer_mean` over all 300 content-addressed clean item records for each displayed Full300 state. Every referenced item was present. The independent final audit is the integrity authority for the full result tree; the table derivation is a descriptive secondary analysis.
