## Findings in brief

At optimizer step 10,000, C2/C3/C6 had much larger observed first-key sink mass and delete/relocate output costs than C0/C1/C4/C5 across all three text domains. On max40 SST-2, for example, their equal-item sink mass was `0.4509/0.5145/0.4746`, with deletion `ΔCE = 0.8695/0.7003/0.6223` nats per target. The corresponding C0 sink mass was `0.0917` and deletion `ΔCE = 0.1023`. On max128 HumanEval prompts, C2/C3/C6 had sink mass `0.3260/0.3794/0.3499` and deletion `ΔCE = 0.2534/0.1875/0.2048`; C0 had `0.0113` and `0.0191`. These are sensitivity measurements for the specified attention edit, not evidence that a sink improves clean prediction or transfers through one unique mechanism.

The context comparison is domain dependent. The same 100 documents in each domain were rendered at max40 and max128, yet the number of scored targets changes. Longer renderings reduced the pooled token-weighted clean CE at the final checkpoint for every condition (for example, C2 `4.9269` to `4.1533`). The equal-document paired **change in intervention cost** was near zero on short SST-2 sentences, generally negative on GSM8K questions, and mixed for HumanEval deletion: C0 and C1 had positive HumanEval delete changes, whereas C2-C6 had negative ones. These context comparisons also change which token positions are included; they cannot isolate context length from position or content.

At step 500, max40 equal-item sink mass averaged across the three domains was already `0.3844/0.4603/0.4098` for C2/C3/C6. Their pooled deletion `ΔCE` was only `0.0738/0.0689/0.0378`, compared with `0.5407/0.4239/0.3907` at 10,000. This supports the descriptive distinction between an established pattern and the later observed cost of this intervention. No binary onset or equivalence threshold was approved, and the four measured checkpoints do not locate an exact onset.

## Design, definitions, and setup

The [S6 study](../../design/e6a_v2/studies/S6_CROSS_DOMAIN.md), [metric contract](../../design/e6a_v2/METRICS_AND_ANALYSIS.md), and [intervention contract](../../design/e6a_v2/MODEL_AND_INTERVENTION_CONTRACTS.md) specify a seed0-only follow-up using retained S1 C0-C6 student checkpoints at optimizer steps **0, 500, 2,000, and 10,000**. The students have GPT-2-medium architecture (24 layers, 16 heads, width 1,024) and were initialized from configuration, not pretrained weights; their frozen S1 teacher was GPT-2-large (36 layers, 20 heads, width 1,280). S1 training used a common 64 × 128-token effective batch, microbatch 4 × accumulation 16, through 10,000 optimizer updates. S6 loaded the frozen student states only and ran FP32, eager-attention inference on Adrita-PC (`DESKTOP-GPO5752`), RTX 4080 SUPER `GPU-2a5c25d0-1f73-919b-fd8b-f6f0df709aaf`. The immutable S6 execution source commit was `0f84307e63c2e76f539f13f97315433580dc6fa7`; this later report does not change that execution identity.

The battery used 100 pinned SST-2 validation **sentences**, 100 GSM8K test **questions**, and 100 HumanEval **prompts**, each rendered from the same document at maximum 40 and 128 real tokens, with right padding and at least two real tokens. There were seven conditions × four states × three domains × two renderings × 100 documents × three operations = **50,400** item-operation observations. Each of the 168 domain/context/state aggregates contains its clean, delete, and relocate operation summaries. The clean and edited scores are next-token causal language-modeling measurements; they are **not** sentiment classification accuracy, GSM8K answer accuracy, HumanEval pass@k, or generated-code execution. No answer, completion, or test-code fields were put into the model inputs. Optional 512/1024 contexts were disabled.

The clean operation leaves attention unchanged. Deletion redistributes first-key probability over valid non-sink keys; relocation moves that probability to key 1. Both use full strength over all 24 native student layers, retaining causal masks and the q0 exception. `ΔCE = CE_edited − CE_clean` is signed, in natural-log nats per valid next-token target; positive values mean lower likelihood of the true targets after editing. `S` is the equal-item mean of later-query attention mass on key 0, averaged across heads and all native layers. Self-KL is clean-to-edited output KL, and flips are the fraction of valid next-token positions whose top-1 prediction changed. These three response measurements need not rank conditions identically. The central table's CE, ΔCE, KL, and flips are **token-weighted** within each domain/context; its `S` is **equal-item**. Each domain has 100 items. Pooled trajectory values below weight all 300 documents by valid targets. The paired-context table gives each of the 100 matched documents equal weight.

## Interpretation boundaries

The primary observation is an association across these fixed trained models: attention-sink pattern and sensitivity to first-key deletion/relocation often co-occur at 10,000 updates, but clean language-modeling quality, structure, and perturbation response need not order conditions the same way. The interventions alter all layers and are causal for their own forward pass, but a changed output is not by itself evidence of the sink's benefit to ordinary prediction, inherited circuit identity, or mediation by a unique route. The domain inputs and scored token spans change between max40 and max128. Consequently, the paired differences show context-rendering sensitivity of this **battery**, not a pure length effect or domain-task performance.

S6 is a D24 **designated-seed0 follow-up** and cannot support across-training-seed reproducibility claims. Panel items, domains, token positions, checkpoints, or hardware are not additional training seeds. Original S1 conditions used different physical GPUs, notably C4 on RTX 3090; those hardware roles remain a confound for cross-condition attribution. The accepted C3 transferred-log prefix gap limits historical training-trajectory provenance but did not weaken its verified checkpoint or S6 result checks. No optional 512/1024 work, HumanEval code execution, S1 training, S2 rerun, or Stage09 work is part of these results.
