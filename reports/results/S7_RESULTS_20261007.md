## Findings in brief

The step-10,000 teacher–student **full attention JSD** from the new FP32 S7 supplement was `0.22881` for C1, `0.05040` for C2, `0.18768` for C5, and `0.06683` for C6 nats/query. The corresponding sink/rest mass components were `0.16069/0.00968/0.14604/0.00732`. Relative to the logit-KD base C1, C2 improved full matching by `0.17841` nats/query, with a signed `0.15101` mass component and `0.02740` weighted shape component. C6 improved full matching by `0.16198`, with `0.15337` from mass and `0.00861` from shape. These are exact additive *attention-divergence* contrasts, not fractions of behavioral improvement or optimization effort.

Replacing C2's full attention target with C5's conditional non-sink target changed the pattern markedly. At 10,000, C5 minus C2 was `+0.13727` full JSD, almost entirely `+0.13636` sink/rest mass JSD, while weighted shape differed by `+0.00092`. C5 nevertheless had the lower **conditional** non-sink JSD (`0.06375` versus C2 `0.07532`). Its recorded clean CE and teacher KL were also lower by `0.01505` and `0.00912` nats/target, respectively. Those fixed-model point differences do not establish equivalence, noninferiority, or population utility.

C6 versus C1 was similarly informative in the other direction: adding binary sink/rest supervision reduced full JSD by `0.16198`, while recorded clean CE was `0.00464` nats/target higher and teacher KL `0.00635` higher. The already-recorded all-layer deletion cost increased by `0.17605` nats/target and relocation cost by `0.26854`. The distinct rankings for attention pattern, clean prediction, and sensitivity to first-key edits answer separate S7 questions. They do not identify a single transferred circuit or a causal mediation pathway.

## Exact attention-divergence decomposition

For the head-mean teacher/student clean attention distributions at a valid query, let `a` and `b` be their key-0 mass. S7 partitions the same full-map Jensen–Shannon divergence in two **different** exact ways:

`D_full = D_mass + D_shape = D_key0 + D_other_columns`.

`D_mass` compares the binary sink-versus-rest allocations; `D_shape` is residual-mass-weighted non-sink shape disagreement. `D_key0` is the literal key-0 coordinate contribution to full JSD. `D_other_columns` covers the other key coordinates. The separately reported symmetric **conditional** non-sink JSD renormalizes non-sink probabilities and is not an additive component of full JSD. In particular, subtracting conditional JSD from full JSD would not measure a sink contribution. All terms use the same 300 items, 24 mapped layers and valid-query support. Lower JSD means closer attention distributions by that particular descriptive metric.

| Condition | Key-0 column JSD | Other-column JSD | Sink/rest mass JSD | Weighted shape JSD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| C1 | 0.11252 | 0.11629 | 0.16069 | 0.06812 |
| C2 | 0.00476 | 0.04564 | 0.00968 | 0.04072 |
| C5 | 0.10040 | 0.08727 | 0.14604 | 0.04164 |
| C6 | 0.00350 | 0.06334 | 0.00732 | 0.05952 |

At step 10,000, the maximum observed `|D_full − D_mass − D_shape|` across every item/layer in the complete 36-state campaign was `3.400058012914542e-16` nats/query; the independent audit found **zero** undefined conditional layer values. The tiny closure residual is a numerical diagnostic, not an effect size. The report preserves both partitions because a mass term includes the complementary residual allocation as well as key 0; it need not equal the literal key-0 column term.

## Interpretation boundary

S7 addresses three distinct observations: **sink/pattern transfer** from clean teacher/student attention, **distillation utility** from existing clean next-token/teacher-matching records, and **inference dependence** from existing first-key delete/relocate records. The exact JSD mass/shape identity decomposes descriptive attention mismatch; it does not decompose a behavioral treatment effect or measure the fraction of training compute allocated to the sink. Higher intervention cost is evidence of output sensitivity under the named edit and mask; it does not prove the sink's ordinary predictive benefit or identify every possible circuit. The source models are GPT-2-large → randomly initialized GPT-2-medium under the fixed OpenWebText recipe and 10,000-update horizon.

Only training seed0 was used. Neither 300 item pairs, 36 checkpoints, 101 Dense64 time points nor three physical training GPUs are additional training-seed replicates. No across-seed reproducibility, population confidence interval, p-value, equivalence, noninferiority, causal mediation, or favorable-checkpoint selection claim follows from these data. The existing three-GPU condition confound and absent S7 no-op/key1 controls further bound cross-recipe and causal interpretation. No S1/S5 input file was modified; no training, seed1/2 inference, S2, Stage09, or new intervention study was run for this report.
