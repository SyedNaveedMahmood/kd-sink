**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-10-07 UTC. **Scope:** the 21 completed condition/seed numeric records in `D:\KD-SINK-central\runs`, plus the seven previously verified complete seed0 checkpoint trees. This is a descriptive results report, not a new protocol lock or a Stage09 release. Its tables were computed read-only by [`scripts/report_s1_c0_c6.py`](../../scripts/report_s1_c0_c6.py) from the archive-validation receipt and the run records. The derived table inventory is `D:\KD-SINK-central\analysis\S1_C0_C6_REPORT_DERIVED_20261007.json` (SHA-256 `8506c1289f25b188b8a7f8c7e013369eede4ba0c44dc5e26793dd5dc2288253f`).

## Main findings

All seven C0–C6 recipes have three recorded training seeds through optimizer step 10,000. Every run has 101 Dense64, nine Full300, and two LM2000 evaluation steps; each contributes 59,206 evaluation JSON files. The 21-run numeric coverage is complete under the compact-archive audit. The exact transferred C3 seed0 training log lacks updates 1–2,500; this accepted historical limitation remains visible below.

At the Full300 endpoint, C2, C3, and C6 have high first-position sink mass (`S` about 0.33–0.40) in every seed. C0, C1, C4, and C5 remain much lower (`S` about 0.009–0.037). C2/C3/C6 also have substantially larger deletion and relocation effects on next-token cross-entropy and predictions. This supports a measured association of sink pattern with *intervention sensitivity* through 10,000 updates. It does not by itself prove that the sink improves clean prediction, that a particular circuit mediates the effect, or that all possible sink interventions would behave alike.

On the same seed-specific Full300 panels, the C1–C6 clean CE values are close relative to the C0-to-C1 improvement; attention objectives separate the sink and intervention results more strongly than clean CE. C5, which removes direct sink supervision from its attention loss, remains low-sink with comparatively modest intervention effects despite competitive clean CE. C4 has low sink mass but nonzero intervention sensitivity. These are descriptive measurements from only three training seeds, with objective/device and panel limitations detailed below. No p-values, equivalence margins, noninferiority claims, or across-population inference are supplied.

## Scientific setup and registered measurements

The frozen teacher is GPT-2-large (36 layers, 20 heads, width 1280). The student is GPT-2-medium architecture (24 layers, 16 heads, width 1024), initialized randomly from configuration, not pretrained weights. Training used the pinned OpenWebText source `Skylion007/openwebtext` revision `79d93d786212f7344586290adb811d4ae6a1762c`, GPT-2 tokenizer revision `607a30d783dfa663caf39e06633721c8d4cfcd7e`, sequence length 128, one independent explicit condition/seed command, and a frozen GPT-2-large teacher. The approved common schedule was microbatch 4 × accumulation 16 = 64 sequences per optimizer update; each update presented 8,192 input tokens and scored 8,128 shifted targets. All endpoints record 81,920,000 input tokens and 81,280,000 shifted targets after 10,000 updates. Presentations include repeated data epochs; the token totals are not unique-token counts.

AdamW used peak LR `5e-4`, betas `(0.9, 0.95)`, epsilon `1e-8`, weight decay `0.1`, gradient clipping at norm `1`, 500-update warmup, then cosine decay to the 10% floor (`5e-5` at step 10,000). The recorded starts show BF16 autocast with FP32 student/master parameters, Adam states, and probability/loss reductions under the [training contract](../../design/e6a_v2/TRAINING_AND_CHECKPOINTING.md). Student dropout was `0.1`; the teacher was in evaluation mode. The approved base for C1–C6 was `0.5 CE + 0.5 logit KD` at training KD temperature 2; evaluation teacher KL is separately measured at temperature 1. The auxiliary coefficient was `1/9`, with the prespecified C3/C4 calibration scales. Raw objective losses are not interchangeable fidelity measures.

The exact objective definitions and scales are in [`OBJECTIVES.md`](../../design/e6a_v2/OBJECTIVES.md). Clean/delete/relocate were recorded for Dense64/Full300 for both student and teacher; LM2000 is student clean/NLL only. `S` is the native-layer mean first-key attention mass over the registered later-query support; full JSD is teacher–student head-mean attention divergence. `ΔCE` is edited minus clean next-token CE in nats per valid target. Positive ΔCE means the edit worsened target likelihood. Self-KL is clean-to-edited and the flip fraction records changed top-1 predictions. [Metric definitions](../../design/e6a_v2/METRICS_AND_ANALYSIS.md) specify the pooling and masks.

## Longitudinal development through 10,000 updates

The table samples the student Dense64 `S` at registered optimizer steps, averaging the three displayed seed-specific values at each step. 1. **Pattern:** C2/C3/C6 have much greater first-key mass and lower teacher–student full-attention JSD than C1/C5 in every seed.
